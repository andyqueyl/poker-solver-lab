How this was solved: all-in equities for all 169 × 169 starting-hand matchups were estimated from 100,000 random boards each, with card removal counted exactly. Fictitious play then found the equilibrium at every stack from 1 to 25bb in half-big-blind steps. Heads-up, the small blind posts 0.5bb, the big blind 1bb, and there are no antes. "Max stack" is the largest stack at which a hand is still a shove or call at every smaller stack.

6-max model: all six players start with the same stack. The action folds to the shover, who shoves or folds. Players behind then call or fold in turn. At most one player calls: once someone calls, everyone else folds. Multiway all-ins are rare in push/fold play, so this changes ranges only a little. With a BB ante, the big blind posts a 1bb ante before its blind. The ante is dead money: the winner takes it, but it does not count toward calling, so the big blind plays for its stack minus 1bb. At 2bb or less with an ante, the big blind is already all-in, so its chart shows a call with every hand. Card removal is exact between the shover and the caller. The model ignores the cards of players who folded. Each shover seat was solved to within 0.001bb of equilibrium for every player at every stack. With no ante, the small blind spot is the heads-up game, and its ranges match the heads-up charts exactly.
