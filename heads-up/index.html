Start from

Starting from the flop lets the solver play preflop for both seats and deals only hands that see a flop, so you can drill postflop play and bet sizing. Each stack depth is a separately solved game; a change takes effect on the next hand.

How the bot was solved: Monte Carlo counterfactual regret minimization on a simplified game. Bet sizes are limited to the buttons you see. After the flop, hands are grouped by their equity against a random hand and how much that equity can swing on later cards (50 groups on the flop and turn, 30 on the river). Preflop uses all 169 starting hands. Each stack depth (100, 40 and 20bb) is solved as its own game; at 40bb and 20bb the only 4-bet is all-in, and at 20bb the 3-bet is to 7. The EV numbers are averages the solver measured while training, so rarely reached spots are less precise.
