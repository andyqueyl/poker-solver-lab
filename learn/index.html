The game: the small blind (who is also the button) shoves all-in or folds; the big blind calls or folds. Exploitability is how much a perfect opponent could win against the strategy, averaged over both seats; zero means equilibrium. CFR updates each hand's regret for every action by how much better that action would have done. CFR+ clips negative regret at zero and weights recent iterations more, which is why it converges much faster. Fictitious play just best-responds to the opponent's average. All-in equities come from 100,000 simulated boards per matchup, with card removal counted.
