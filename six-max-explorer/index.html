This explorer reads the same postflop solutions as the 6-Max Hold'em Trainer. Each pot type was solved with Monte Carlo CFR starting from the flop, with each player's preflop range taken from the 6-max preflop solution (averaged over the seat combinations that lead to that kind of pot). Bet sizes are a third or three quarters of the pot heads-up and half the pot while three players are in. Postflop hands are grouped by equity against a random hand and how much it can swing. "Hands in range" weights each combo by its preflop range weight and how often the player takes the actions so far; bar heights are relative to the most common hand. EVs are training averages for the player to act, in big blinds from the start of the hand.
