Hand detail

This explorer reads the same solution as the Heads-Up Hold'em Trainer: Monte Carlo CFR on a simplified game with the bet sizes shown, the stack depth you pick (each depth is solved as its own game), and postflop hands grouped by equity against a random hand and how much it can swing (50 groups on the flop and turn, 30 on the river). Every combo in a group gets the same strategy, so suits only matter through the groups. "Hands in range" weights each combo by how often the player reaches this point with it, given the actions so far. EVs are the solver's training averages for the player to act, in big blinds from the start of the hand.
