Join a room

How it works. The person who creates the room is the host: their browser deals and runs the betting, and sends each player only their own cards. Each card is drawn at random from the undealt cards at the moment it's dealt, so the flop, turn and river don't exist until their street comes. The host's browser does hold everyone's hole cards during a hand, so play with a host you trust. Everyone else connects straight to the host's browser with the room code, after a free public matchmaking service introduces them. There's no game server and no account. Rooms are private: only people with the code can join, and the host can remove anyone. Chips are play money only. If the host reloads the page, the room comes back with the same code and players reconnect on their own.
